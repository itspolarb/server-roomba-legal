Command guide

You do not need to memorize dozens of commands. Start with /help; Discord will show each subcommand and its required options as you type.

Start a new server setup

/setup quickstart

Choose Community, Gaming/RP, Creator/marketplace, or Custom/manual. Presets adjust modules and basic protection settings; they do not create channels, roles, filters, policies, or panels.

/setup doctor

Checks permissions, role hierarchy, configured channels, tickets, verification, AutoMod, and other enabled features.

/health

Shows whether Server Roomba is ready and points managers to permissions, setup, or delivery problems that need attention.

/module list

Shows all 20 modules and whether each one is running.

Verification and the optional CAPTCHA

Members verify through a panel posted by server managers. The panel can be a simple Verify button or a versioned rules-and-terms message that records acceptance. Servers may also require Discord membership screening, a minimum account age, staff review, or a private CAPTCHA.

/verify configure

Sets the verified and unverified roles, review channel, minimum account age, membership-screening requirement, and optional CAPTCHA. Use captcha:True to turn on the human check for this server.

/verify policy

Publishes the current rules, terms, optional disclaimer, and acceptance button. Publishing a new version intentionally disables older policy buttons.

/verify panel

Posts a simpler verification button when the server does not need versioned policy acceptance.

/verify status

Shows the active roles, checks, CAPTCHA setting, review queue, and current panel or policy version.

/verify pending /verify acceptance

Lets managers review waiting accounts and confirm whether a member accepted the active policy.

/verify approve /verify deny /verify reset

Handles staff-assisted verification and recovery. Staff approval can assist a member without requiring them to solve the CAPTCHA.

What the CAPTCHA does: it presents a short randomized arithmetic challenge in a private Discord window. Challenges expire after five minutes, and repeated wrong answers cause a temporary lock. It is a practical deterrent for basic automated joins—not identity verification or a guarantee that a member is trustworthy.

Meme vault

The Meme vault is server-local and optional. Submitted images stay in a private review channel until a bot manager approves them. Every server controls its own collection, tags, submissions, and enabled sources.

/meme random

Posts a random meme from an enabled source. Choose a source, filter the local vault by tag, or make the result private.

/meme submit

Submits a PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP for review with an optional caption and up to five tags.

/meme mine

Shows your recent submissions and their review status.

/meme configure

Manager command that selects the private review/storage channel and opens or closes member submissions.

/meme sources

Shows which meme sources are available and enabled. The local vault, XKCD, PG-rated GIPHY, and an allowlisted SFW Lemmy feed are supported when available.

/meme source-enable /meme source-disable

Lets managers decide which available sources members can use.

/meme pending /meme approve /meme reject /meme remove

Reviews the queue, records decisions, and removes entries that no longer belong in the collection.

/meme status

Shows the vault channel, submission state, approved and pending totals, and enabled sources.

External does not mean unfiltered. Sources are allowlisted, unsafe metadata is rejected where available, provider links are validated, and managers can disable any source. The reviewed local vault remains the safest choice for tightly controlled communities.

Counting channels

Counting keeps progress across restarts, prevents the same member from counting twice in a row, and handles mistakes without deleting the original message. A wrong count receives a red ❌ and an explanation; managers choose whether that mistake resets progress.

/counting setup channel:#channel reset_on_fail:True

Enables counting in the selected channel. Use reset_on_fail:False when mistakes should be marked and explained without resetting progress.

/counting setup channel:#new-channel confirm_reset:True

Moves counting to another channel. Confirmation is required because changing channels resets the saved count.

/counting status

Shows whether counting is enabled, its channel, current number, and reset-on-failure setting.

/counting history

Shows recent resets, the previous number, who or what caused each reset, and when it happened.

/counting reset confirm:True

Manually resets progress to zero. The next valid message is 1.

/counting disable

Stops processing new counts while preserving the saved progress for later.

How modules behave

Disabling a module stops its commands and event processing but keeps its configuration. Re-enable it later and the setup is still there. Server Roomba currently includes 20 modules covering AutoMod, moderation, tickets, autoroles, role menus, raid protection, logging, welcomes, verification, birthdays, tags, suggestions, polls, automation, levels, giveaways, starboard, counting, tabletop dice, and the Meme vault.

Role hierarchy still wins. Discord will not let the bot manage a member or role equal to or above Server Roomba’s highest role.
