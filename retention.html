Data lifecycle

Retention Schedule

Effective July 25, 2026

This schedule states Server Roomba’s default retention periods. A legal hold, security incident, or valid legal obligation may require a temporary documented exception.

RecordDefault retention
Completed scheduled jobs30 days
Automation executions90 days
AutoMod violation counters90 days
Entries for ended giveaways90 days
Configuration history1 year
Starboard tracking records1 year
Active configuration and policiesUntil server-owner deletion
Moderation cases and ticketsUntil server-owner deletion
Removed-server data30-day grace period, then deletion
Daily database backups14 days

Member and server-owner controls

Members can use /privacy data, /privacy export, and /privacy delete. Server owners can use the guarded /setup delete-data command.

Questions about a retention exception or deletion request can be sent to thepolarbearr@gmail.com.
