Agreement

By inviting, configuring, or using Server Roomba, you agree to these Terms and the Privacy Policy. You must comply with Discord’s terms, community guidelines, and applicable law. If you configure the bot for a server, you confirm that you are authorized to do so.

Server and user responsibilities

Server managers are responsible for role hierarchy, permissions, enabled modules, automated punishments, retention choices, link allowlists and blocklists, verification text, and any content submitted through tags, embeds, tickets, feedback, or automations.

Do not use Server Roomba for harassment, unlawful surveillance, unauthorized access, spam, evading Discord safeguards, distributing malicious content, or knowingly creating deceptive security rules.

Public beta

Server Roomba is under active development. Features, commands, limits, storage formats, and availability may change. Reasonable care is taken to preserve server configuration and history during upgrades, but uninterrupted or error-free operation is not guaranteed.

Availability and enforcement limits

The service depends on Discord, hosting providers, third-party content sources, network availability, and the permissions granted in each server. A moderation or safety action can fail when Discord denies access, a role is too high, a channel is hidden, or an external source is unavailable.

Automated detection—including phishing, invite, raid, spam, and AutoMod checks—reduces risk but cannot identify every harmful item or avoid every false positive. Server managers should test policies and maintain human review.

Suspension, removal, and changes

Access may be limited or removed to protect the service, comply with law or Discord requirements, address abuse, or perform maintenance. You may stop using the service by removing the bot; authorized server controls should be used first if you want server data deleted.

Material updates will be published here with a new effective date. Questions may be sent to thepolarbearr@gmail.com.
